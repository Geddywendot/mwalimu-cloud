# 3-Minute Demo Script: Mwalimu Cloud (Curated-Content Edition)

**Presenter Note:** Speak clearly, speak with pride in African context, and maintain complete transparency about the AI-assisted, human-reviewed content curation process.

---

### [0:00 - 0:25] The Problem & Introduction
> "Jambo! Studying for the Microsoft Azure Fundamentals exam (AZ-900) often feels daunting. The documentation is full of abstract corporate jargon, and most study platforms assume you live next to a Western enterprise datacenter with unlimited high-speed broadband.
> 
> We built **Mwalimu Cloud** — an unofficial study buddy designed for learners across Africa. It explains Azure concepts through things you already know, offers 120+ original scenario questions, and works completely offline with zero API keys or server costs."

---

### [0:25 - 0:50] Instant Guest Start & Honesty Badges
> "Notice we don't force learners to create accounts or pay for an AI key. I tap **Continue as guest**, and I'm immediately in.
>
> In the header, you will see a badge that says **Curated content**. We believe in radical honesty: this edition uses pre-written, AI-assisted, human-reviewed content rather than unpredictable live AI calls. If we toggle to Kiswahili, you will also see a badge noting that translations are under ongoing native-speaker review."

---

### [0:50 - 1:35] Explain Mode: African Analogies & Follow-Up Chips
> "Let's open **Benefits of cloud services**.
>
> Instead of just dry definitions, Mwalimu Cloud explains that *Scalability & Elasticity* are like a matatu route: adding vehicles during rush hour and parking them at midday so you only pay for what you use.
>
> But we never let the analogy replace the truth. Every card displays the official definition side by side with the analogy, plus a crucial section: **'Where it breaks down'** — noting that unlike matatus, cloud servers can spin up automatically in seconds across continents.
>
> If I need it even plainer, I toggle **Explain it simpler**. And below, instead of a broken chatbot, I have tappable **Common Questions** chips that reveal answers to edge cases instantly."

---

### [1:35 - 2:15] Quiz Engine: Deep Explanations & Reporting
> "Now let's test our retention with **Quiz me on this**.
>
> I choose a 5-question quiz. Options are shuffled every session to prevent rote memorization. Let's submit an answer.
>
> Notice the feedback panel: it doesn't just say 'Wrong' or 'Correct'. It explains why the right answer is right, and provides a distinct explanation for **every single wrong option**. If a question feels questionable, a learner can tap **Report this question** to flag it for audit."

---

### [2:15 - 2:45] Spaced Repetition, Dashboard & Study Planner
> "Missed questions aren't forgotten. They automatically feed into our **Leitner Spaced Repetition engine**, appearing in **Today's Revision** at 1, 3, 7, and 14-day intervals until mastered.
>
> On the **Readiness Dashboard**, learners see their accuracy mapped against official AZ-900 domain weights, a progress trend line, and an exam countdown with daily question targets.
>
> And for learners on a timeline, our **Study Planner** generates an adaptive, day-by-day roadmap prioritizing their weakest areas."

---

### [2:45 - 3:00] Offline PWA & Closing
> "Best of all: Mwalimu Cloud is an offline Progressive Web App. A learner can load this once on their phone, take the bus home without data, and keep studying.
>
> Mwalimu Cloud proves that world-class cloud education doesn't require expensive AI subscriptions — just thoughtful pedagogy, cultural resonance, and rigorous engineering. As noted on every page: Unofficial study tool. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Microsoft. Asante sana!"
